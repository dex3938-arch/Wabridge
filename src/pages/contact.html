Before you write

Can you install WaBridge for us?

Choose “Installation help” in the form and describe your computer or server. We will reply with the options available for your setup.

Can I try it with a test number first?

That is exactly what we recommend. Link a spare number, invite a teammate and try campaigns on a few friends who agreed to help before you connect your main business number.

What information should I include?

Your industry, roughly how many WhatsApp chats you handle per day, how many people reply, and whether you already have a Meta Business account. Please do not send passwords or API keys.
