Retail, boutiques & food

From “is this available?” to a confirmed order

Stores and home bakers get the same five questions all day — availability, price, delivery, payment and timings. WaBridge answers the repeat ones instantly and routes real orders to a person.

  • Keyword replies for price, size, delivery areas and store timings
  • Templates for UPI details, address and order-shipped updates
  • Festive campaigns for Diwali, Eid or end-of-season sales to opted-in buyers
  • Tags such as “vip” or “pune” to target the right customers next time

Coaching & education

Parents get answers, teachers get their evenings back

Batch timings, fee dates and demo-class bookings arrive on WhatsApp at all hours. Set them up once and let WaBridge reply, with a teacher stepping in only when needed.

  • Away messages after class hours that say exactly when you will reply
  • Fee and exam reminders sent as paced campaigns to opted-in parents
  • AI answers from your syllabus, batch list and fee structure
  • Separate numbers for admissions and current students

Agencies & freelancers

Offer WhatsApp automation to every client

Install one WaBridge per client on a small server, link their numbers, and manage campaigns and auto-replies for them — without per-message charges eating into your margin.

  • Client-owned data on infrastructure you control
  • Signed webhooks into n8n, Zapier-style tools or your own reports
  • Engine API explorer for groups, channels, labels and status updates
  • Official API option for clients who need approved templates

Typical setup: one Linux server with 4 GB RAM can comfortably host a WaBridge for each of several small clients, each with its own login and numbers.

Customer support

Turn one busy phone into a support desk

Order status, returns and complaints need follow-through. WaBridge gives support teams queues, ownership and history — like a helpdesk, but on WhatsApp.

  • Pending queue for chats waiting on the customer or a pickup
  • Human hand-off the moment someone types “agent” or “call me”
  • Median first-reply time on the dashboard to keep the team honest

WaBridge is not intended for regulated messaging such as healthcare records or financial transactions — use Meta's official Cloud API for those.
