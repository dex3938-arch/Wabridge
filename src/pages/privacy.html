Privacy notice

Last updated {{updated}}

This notice explains what happens to information on this website. It does not cover the WaBridge software you install yourself — that software stores its data on your own computer or server, under your control.

What we collect

The website itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics. If you use the contact form, we receive what you type: your name, WhatsApp number, e-mail address (if given), the topic and your message.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your request and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to marketing broadcasts unless you separately agree.

Where it goes

Form messages are delivered either by e-mail or into the site owner's own WaBridge workspace. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security for a limited time.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, and then deleted or anonymised.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details you sent, and you can reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time to stop messages from us.{{#if email}} Write to {{email}}.{{/if}}

Security

The site is served over HTTPS with a strict content security policy, and the contact form uses spam protection that does not track you.
